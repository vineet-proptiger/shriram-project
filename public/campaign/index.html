Pudhiya Chennai — Where Today’s Smart Price Becomes Tomorrow’s Big Growth Address.

Shriram Codename Pudhiya Chennai is a thoughtfully planned residential development near Poonamallee, located in Chennai’s fastest-growing west corridor. Spread across 8.9 acres, the project offers well-designed 2 and 3 BHK homes with 75% open spaces, ensuring a balanced lifestyle. With 7 towers, 1,053 apartments, and a 12,000 sq. ft. clubhouse featuring 40+ modern amenities, it blends comfort with future-ready living. Excellent connectivity to the upcoming metro, Bengaluru Expressway, and proposed airport infrastructure makes it ideal for both end-users and long-term investors.
